Inquiries

What materials do you use? +
We use responsibly sourced hardwood selected for grain quality and durability. Engravings are done with precision laser technology and finished by hand with eco-conscious sealants.
Can I customize my order? +
Yes, customization is one of our core offerings. You can request names, wording, dimensions, finishes, and styling preferences to perfectly suit your intended space.
What is your shipping timeline? +
Most standard pieces are completed in 5-7 business days. Personalized projects generally take 10-14 business days depending on design complexity.
